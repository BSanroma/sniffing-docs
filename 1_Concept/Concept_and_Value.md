# 1. Concept & Value Proposition

## The Problem: The Collapse of Passive Repositories
Since the invention of the printing press, humanity has stored knowledge in static formats. Modern digital repositories, despite their processing speed, still inherit this structural limitation: they are passive archives of inert documents that require proactive human action to be retrieved, analyzed, and connected. 

With approximately 2.5 million new scientific papers published annually, researchers face severe **information overload**. The sheer volume of literature makes manual identification of knowledge gaps, detection of redundancies, and discovery of cross-disciplinary innovations practically impossible. 

## The Sniffing Solution
Sniffing is a generative research engine designed to solve this crisis by transforming a passive repository into a **living, collaborative ecosystem**. 

### How it Works
1. **Knowledge Extraction:** Sniffing processes scientific papers to extract their **Ontological Identity** (what they are), **Epistemic Contributions** (what they solve), and **Dimensional Gaps / Needs** (what they leave open).
2. **Semantic Matching (The "Sniff"):** The system uses vector embeddings and advanced similarity search (like pgvector) to connect the *Needs* of one paper with the *Contributions* of another. This allows papers from completely different fields (e.g., Paleontology and Neuroscience) to connect conceptually, even if they share no keywords or citations.
3. **The Dialogue Table:** Once a match is established, the papers are brought into an active Dialogue Table. Here, autonomous AI agents (LLMs acting with specific researcher personas) debate the intersection of knowledge to generate new hypotheses, validate connections, and synthesize cross-pollinated insights.

## Value Proposition
- **For Researchers & Academics:** Accelerates the ideation phase, automates literature reviews, and discovers non-obvious cross-disciplinary connections.
- **For R&D Departments:** Breaks down corporate information silos, connecting past internal research with external state-of-the-art literature to solve current technical challenges.
- **For the Scientific Community:** Promotes lateral thinking and generative innovation, moving beyond simple search to active knowledge creation.
